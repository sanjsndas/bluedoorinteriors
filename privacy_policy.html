Privacy Policy

Blue Door Interiors Privacy Policy

Blue Door Interiors (“we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal data when you visit our website, contact us or use our interior design services in England.

Personal data we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, contact details, information about your design enquiry or project, communications you send us, and information you provide when arranging or receiving our services. When you use our website, we may also collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and information about how you use the site, including through cookies or similar technologies.

How we use your data

We may use personal data to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, provide and manage our services, communicate with you, process payments where applicable, maintain business records, improve our website and services, and meet our legal obligations.

Our lawful bases

Under UK data protection law, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract and performing a contract with you; our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business and services, provided those interests do not override your rights; your consent, where required; and compliance with legal obligations.

Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with service providers who help us operate our business, such as website, IT, payment or administrative providers, and with professional advisers or public authorities where necessary or legally required. We expect recipients to protect your data and use it only for appropriate purposes. If personal data is transferred outside the UK, we will use a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards where required.

How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, manage our business and meet legal, accounting or reporting requirements. The appropriate retention period depends on the type of data and why we hold it.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies. Some may be necessary for the site to function; others may help us understand site usage or improve your experience. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and, where available, our website’s cookie controls.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to its processing, or request its transfer. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw consent at any time; this will not affect processing carried out before withdrawal. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data. However, no method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure.

Children

Our services and website are not intended to collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on our website with its updated date.

Contact us

For questions about this policy or to exercise your data protection rights, contact Blue Door Interiors using the contact details provided on our website.
